# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This repository is a space for sharing engineering work, learning, technical discussion, and collaboration. Participation is expected to be respectful, constructive, and safe for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat others with courtesy and respect, including when opinions or technical approaches differ.
- Keep criticism focused on code, evidence, design choices, and ideas—not on a person's identity or worth.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith. Explain disagreements professionally and welcome reasonable corrections.
- Credit original authors and sources. Be transparent about your own contributions, test results, and the maturity or limitations of your work.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, consent, and clearly stated communication boundaries.
- Help keep discussions relevant, accessible, and useful to the engineering community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable in this repository or its associated community spaces:

- Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, threats, intimidation, or targeted personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexualised language, imagery, attention, or contact.
- Trolling, persistent disruptive behaviour, or deliberately hostile comments.
- Publishing or misusing another person's private or identifying information without permission.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, false attribution, or deliberately misrepresenting another person's work or contributions.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, or other conduct that materially disrupts constructive participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and any communication space explicitly operated for this repository. It also applies when someone is officially representing this repository or its community in public.

## Reporting a concern

Please report a concern privately by contacting the repository maintainer through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ron-rex).

Do not publish incident details, personal information, screenshots, or other sensitive material in a public issue or pull request. If you cannot contact the maintainer privately through LinkedIn, open an issue titled **"Private Code of Conduct report request"** and include only a request for a private contact method—do not include details of the incident. The maintainer will use that request to arrange a private channel.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer will make a good-faith effort to review reports promptly and fairly, consider relevant context, and protect the privacy of those involved. Depending on the severity and circumstances, corrective action may include a private warning, removal or rejection of content, restrictions on participation, or a temporary or permanent ban from repository spaces.

Enforcement decisions will be proportionate to the behaviour and its impact. Retaliation against a reporter or anyone assisting with a review is not permitted.
